About MarketCentaur

Half machine, half human, by design.

MarketCentaur delivers AI-led transformation for every business. In mythology, a centaur combines two kinds of strength, and we do the same for businesses of every size. AI does the fast, wide research that used to take an analyst weeks, and human judgment decides what's true, what matters and what to do next.

Why we exist

Every business needs good research. Few teams have time for it.

Market research, competitive intelligence, conversion work and automation all take specialist time. Many teams go without, wait in a queue for it, or get generic advice from agencies selling one channel.

AI has changed the economics. Work that once needed a team of analysts can now be done in days. But raw AI output is unreliable and generic. We built MarketCentaur to close that gap: AI-speed research, checked and turned into specific recommendations by people who are accountable for them.

  • Website conversion: turning existing traffic into inquiries and sales
  • B2B intelligence: knowing which accounts to pursue and what to say
  • AI automation: removing repetitive work without losing control
  • Ongoing intelligence: staying ahead of competitors and market shifts
How we work

Four standards every deliverable meets

01

Scope first

We agree the question, deliverables, timeline and price in writing before work starts.

02

Sources shown

Every important finding links to its source, so you can check it yourself.

03

Facts vs. opinions

We label what we observed separately from what we recommend.

04

People approve

A person reviews every AI-assisted output. In automations we build, people approve anything that is sent, published or paid for.
