About MarketCentaur

B2B growth intelligence built around the accounts that matter.

MarketCentaur helps lean B2B teams turn ICP definitions, account research, buying signals and competitive activity into concise intelligence they can act on. We combine structured research with AI-assisted workflows to help teams understand which accounts matter, what is changing around them, who is worth engaging, and why the timing may be relevant.

The problem

More data doesn't automatically create better opportunities.

Growth teams often have access to plenty of company, contact and market data, but still struggle to know which accounts deserve attention, what has changed inside them, and what makes the timing relevant. The result can be long account lists, fragmented research and too much time spent separating useful signals from background noise.

Our approach

Focus the research on decisions.

MarketCentaur organizes research around practical questions: Which accounts fit? What changed? Who matters? Why now? And what should the sales or marketing team do next? The goal is not to produce more information—it is to turn relevant information into a clearer basis for action.

Built for lean B2B teams

Practical intelligence without another layer of marketing noise.

Our services combine structured account research, market monitoring and AI-assisted workflows to deliver prioritized intelligence in a format that supports ABM, outbound, customer growth and competitive decisions. We focus on concise, useful outputs that can fit into existing sales and marketing workflows rather than creating another complicated system for teams to manage.

What we look for

Fit, change, people and timing.

We look beyond static firmographics. Relevant signals can include leadership changes, hiring activity, expansion, product launches, partnerships, competitive moves and other developments that may indicate a shift in an account's priorities.

What we deliver

Context your team can use.

Research is organized into prioritized accounts, relevant contacts, important signals and practical observations. This gives teams a clearer starting point for account planning, personalization, outreach and market decisions.
