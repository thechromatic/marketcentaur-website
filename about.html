About MarketCentaur

See the opportunities your business may be overlooking.

MarketCentaur is an expert-led consulting partner connecting business strategy, applied AI, market intelligence, operations, governance and digital innovation. We help organisations look beyond isolated problems to understand what is changing, where effort is being lost, which decisions need better evidence and where practical improvements may be possible. We start with your context—not a predetermined tool or a one-size-fits-all service.

Find your starting point

The problem

Busy businesses can still miss the bigger picture.

Disconnected tools, repeated manual work, fragmented information and unclear priorities can make it harder to spot the next useful move. The challenge may sit in strategy, customer experience, operations, data, risk or technology—or in the connections between them.

Our approach

Connect insight to action.

We combine structured problem-solving, expert judgement, research and AI-enabled methods to identify options, assess feasibility and shape practical next steps. Depending on the challenge, that may mean a clearer strategy, a more useful intelligence workflow, an improved process, a better decision system or a carefully scoped digital solution.

What we look for

Look across the whole business.

We consider the commercial goal, people and processes involved, available evidence, existing systems, implementation constraints and potential risks. This wider view helps keep recommendations grounded in the reality of how the organisation works.

What we deliver

A clearer path to the next move.

Depending on the scope, work may result in an opportunity assessment, decision brief, prioritised roadmap, process design, automation plan, intelligence system or implementation support. The aim is to help your team understand its options and move forward with greater clarity.
