Short answer

We run six hands-on AI workshops for teams: sales and marketing, leaders, operations, customer support, responsible use, and AI search visibility. Each is tailored after a short discovery session, uses your own material, and runs on-site across India or online. A prompt library and AI-use policy are included.

Workshops

Six workshops, each built on your own tasks

Workshop 01

AI for sales and marketing teams

For: Sales, marketing and business development teams

  • Research accounts and prepare for calls faster
  • Draft outreach, proposals and campaign copy from your own material
  • Check AI output for accuracy before it reaches a customer

You leave with: A prompt library for your products and customers

Workshop 02

AI for leaders

For: Owners, directors and senior managers

  • Where AI fits in your business, and where it does not yet
  • How to choose a first use case and measure it
  • What to govern: data, approvals and accountability

You leave with: A shortlist of use cases and a simple decision framework

Workshop 03

AI for operations and admin

For: Operations, finance and admin teams

  • Map repetitive tasks and spot what can be automated
  • Use AI safely for documents, reports and data clean-up
  • Keep a person in control of every approval

You leave with: A ranked list of tasks to automate first

Workshop 04

AI for customer support

For: Support, service and front-desk teams

  • Draft replies faster without losing your tone
  • Build and maintain an answer library
  • Know when a conversation must go to a person

You leave with: A starter answer library and handoff rules

Workshop 05

Responsible AI use

For: Everyone who uses AI tools at work

  • What data should never go into an AI tool
  • How to check output and spot confident mistakes
  • How your AI-use policy and register work day to day

You leave with: A plain-English AI-use policy and register to adapt

Workshop 06

AI search visibility for marketers

For: Marketing and content teams

  • How ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity choose what to recommend
  • Testing the questions your buyers ask
  • Content and page changes that make you easier to cite

You leave with: A buyer question set and a fix list for your site

How it works

From discovery to follow-up

  • Discovery session. A short call with a few team members to collect real tasks and current tool use.
  • Tailored workshop. Exercises built on your products, customers and documents.
  • Policy and prompts. A prompt library and a plain-English AI-use policy for your team to adapt.
  • Follow-up. A short follow-up to answer questions. Where the team finds a task worth automating, the AI Workflow Automation Sprint is the natural next step.

Frequently asked questions

Are workshops tailored to our company?

Yes. Every workshop starts with a short discovery session with a few of your team members, and the exercises use your real products, customers and tasks.

Where do you run on-site workshops?

On-site sessions run in cities across India, and online sessions run for teams anywhere. Location, dates and group size are agreed in the written scope.

Which AI tools do you teach?

The tools your team already has access to wherever possible. If you have none yet, we cover widely available assistants and explain what to check before adopting one.
