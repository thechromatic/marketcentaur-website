How to read them

Every finding says what kind of finding it is.

Each report opens with a bottom line and ends with confidence notes and caveats. In between, every point is labelled:

Observed

What we found

Facts from the website, interviews or public sources. In client work, each one links to its source and date.

Interpretation

What it likely means

Our reading of the evidence. It's judgment, so it's labelled as judgment.

Recommendation

What to do

Ranked actions, with the effort and risk of each, so you can decide what to do first.

Why fictional companies?

Client work stays confidential.

Client reports contain commercial details we don't publish. The sprint samples recreate a realistic situation, so you can judge the structure, depth and honesty of the work before you share anything about your own business. As clients agree, we will add anonymized case studies alongside them.
