Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our sprints, pricing, timelines and how we use AI.

What does MarketCentaur do?

MarketCentaur is an AI-powered growth consultancy. We run short, fixed-scope sprints that fix three common growth problems: websites that don't convert visitors into inquiries, sales teams targeting the wrong accounts, and repetitive work that should be automated. We also take on wider projects across market intelligence, strategy, operations, AI governance and training.

Which sprint should I start with?

If you get website traffic but not enough inquiries or sales, start with the Website Conversion Sprint. If you sell to other businesses and need better targets, start with the B2B Account Intelligence Sprint. If your team loses hours to manual admin, reporting or handoffs, start with the AI Workflow Automation Sprint. Not sure? Tell us your goal and we'll recommend one.

How long does a sprint take?

First findings typically arrive within 48 hours, and the full deliverable in 3–5 business days. Automation sprints take 5–7 business days. Each sprint ends with a walkthrough call.

How much do services cost?

Every engagement is priced as a fixed quote after a free 20-minute scoping call, so you know the full cost before any work starts. Ongoing support is billed monthly and can be canceled with 30 days' notice.

How do you use AI, and who checks the work?

We use AI tools to speed up research, analysis and drafting. A person reviews every finding and recommendation before you see it, and every important claim links to its source. In any automation we build, people approve anything that is sent, published or paid for.

What happens to my data?

We use the information you share, plus publicly available sources, only to deliver your work and respond to you. We don't sell personal information. Tell us if anything you share is confidential and we'll agree how it's handled before work starts. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Who does MarketCentaur work with?

Small and mid-sized businesses worldwide, including local services, ecommerce and D2C brands, professional service firms, B2B technology companies, manufacturers, distributors and agencies. We work remotely with clients in any country.

Does MarketCentaur offer account-based marketing (ABM)?

Yes. Our B2B Account Intelligence Sprint covers ICP definition, target-account research, decision-maker mapping, buying-signal research and recommended outreach angles. Ongoing tracking is available through the Growth Intelligence Retainer.

Can you help with AI search visibility (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity)?

Yes. The Website Conversion Sprint includes a check of how your business appears in AI assistant answers for up to 10 buyer questions, plus recommendations to improve it. Read our sample brief on why this matters.

Do you guarantee leads, sales or rankings?

No one can honestly guarantee those outcomes, because they depend on your market, offer and execution. What we do commit to is a defined scope, specific deliverables and a timeline, all agreed in writing before work starts.

What is the founding-client program?

We're taking on a limited number of founding clients at preferred terms. In exchange, they give permission to publish an anonymized case study of the results. Ask about availability.

How do I get started?

Use the contact page or email hello@marketcentaur.com. We reply within one business day with a suggested starting point. You don't need a complete brief to get in touch.
