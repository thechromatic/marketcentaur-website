Frequently asked questions

Learn how MarketCentaur approaches business strategy, market intelligence, operations, applied AI, governance, digital improvement and ways to begin.

What does MarketCentaur do?

MarketCentaur is an expert-led consulting partner connecting business strategy, market and business intelligence, operations, applied AI and automation, governance and digital improvement. We help organisations identify overlooked opportunities, make better-informed decisions and improve how work gets done. The right approach depends on the challenge, required expertise and implementation context.

Who does MarketCentaur work with?

We work with B2B and B2C organisations, small and local businesses, ecommerce brands and established companies seeking clearer decisions, better processes, stronger customer experiences or new opportunities. Engagements can span strategy, intelligence, operations, technology and customer-facing improvements.

What is B2B growth intelligence?

It organizes target-account fit, company changes, relevant decision-makers, buying signals and competitor activity into concise context that can help sales and marketing teams prioritize actions.

Does MarketCentaur offer account-based marketing?

Yes. Potential ABM support includes ICP definition, target-account research, decision-maker mapping, buying-signal research, campaign planning and account-focused messaging.

Does MarketCentaur work with B2C businesses?

Yes. B2C support can include brand positioning, social content, SEO and local visibility, campaign planning, ecommerce conversion improvements, email marketing, customer research and retention strategy.

Can MarketCentaur help if I am not sure what the problem is?

Yes. You can start with the business outcome you want to improve or describe what feels difficult. We can help clarify the question, identify what evidence may be useful and suggest a practical next step before a wider scope is agreed.

Can one engagement cover more than one business function?

Yes. Some challenges cross strategy, operations, data, customer experience and technology. We can explore the relevant connections and agree a focused scope rather than treating each issue as unrelated.

Does MarketCentaur guarantee leads or search rankings?

No. Marketing outcomes depend on the market, offer, competition, budget, implementation and other factors. Scope, measures of success and deliverables should be agreed for each engagement.

How do I get started?

Use the contact page to describe your business, goals and current challenge. A complete brief is not required to start a conversation.

How much do services cost?

Pricing is not published on the website. Scope and commercial terms can be discussed after understanding your goals and requested support.
