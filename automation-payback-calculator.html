Frequently asked questions

How accurate is this estimate?

It is a planning estimate based on the numbers you enter. Real savings depend on how often the work happens, how much of it follows clear rules and how much review stays with people. An Automation Opportunity Audit measures this properly.

Why does the calculator keep some review time?

Automated work that affects customers, money or legal commitments should be checked by a person. Keeping review time in the estimate gives a more honest figure.

What hourly cost should I use?

Use the full cost of an hour of the person's time: salary plus employer costs such as benefits and taxes, divided by working hours. A rough figure is fine for a first comparison.

Is anything I enter stored or sent anywhere?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is saved or sent.
