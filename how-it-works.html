How it works

A simple process, from business question to useful action.

Every MarketCentaur engagement starts with a clear question, a defined scope and an agreed deliverable. The aim is to make research useful for real decisions—not to add more information to an already busy team.

Discuss your projectView sample report
01 / ALIGN

Define the question

Agree the market, ideal customer profile, intended use and what a useful result should contain.

02 / RESEARCH

Find relevant information

Review the selected companies, roles and market developments against the agreed criteria.

03 / REVIEW

Check the context

Verify important facts, record supporting references and distinguish findings from interpretation.

04 / DELIVER

Make the next step clear

Deliver a concise brief or watchlist with priorities, rationale and practical next actions.

Quality standards

Clear, verifiable and relevant

  • Scope and success criteria agreed up front
  • Source notes for important findings where available
  • Observed facts kept distinct from hypotheses
  • Recommendations connected to your actual use case
Flexible engagement

Start small, expand when useful

Begin with an account map, market brief or partner shortlist. When regular updates add value, the same agreed focus can be extended into an ongoing monitoring service.

Scope, timing and commercial terms are discussed before work begins.

Start with the decision you're trying to make.

You don't need a perfect brief. A short description of your target market and challenge is enough to begin.
