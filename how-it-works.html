How it works

A simple process, from business question to useful action.

Every MarketCentaur engagement starts with the outcome you want to improve, a clear scope and an agreed deliverable. The work may involve research, strategy, process improvement, AI, data or digital experience—the aim is to turn a business question into useful action, not add complexity.

Discuss your projectView sample brief
01 / ALIGN

Define the outcome

Clarify the decision, opportunity or process to improve, who is involved, and what a useful result should make possible.

02 / RESEARCH

Understand the current picture

Review relevant evidence, workflows, customer needs, systems and constraints against the agreed scope.

03 / REVIEW

Assess options and trade-offs

Check important facts, identify assumptions, consider effort and risk, and distinguish evidence from interpretation.

04 / DELIVER

Make the next step clear

Deliver the agreed brief, roadmap, process design or recommendations with priorities, rationale and practical next actions.

Quality standards

Grounded, relevant and practical

  • Scope and success criteria agreed up front
  • Source notes for important findings where available
  • Observed facts kept distinct from hypotheses
  • Recommendations connected to your actual use case
Flexible engagement

Start small, expand when useful

Begin with a focused assessment, research brief, roadmap or workflow review. When continued support adds value, the work can extend into recurring intelligence, implementation coordination or improvement reviews.

Scope, timing and commercial terms are discussed before work begins.
