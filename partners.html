Questions

Partner FAQs

Who can join the partner program?

Agencies, freelancers, consultants, software resellers, IT and web developers, accountants, business coaches, industry associations and anyone who regularly advises businesses that could use our services.

How do referral partners earn?

Referral partners earn a referral fee on the first engagement for every introduction that becomes a paying client. Terms are agreed in writing when you join, and we tell you the status of every introduction.

Will you approach my clients for other work?

No. With white-label partners we never contact your clients directly unless you ask us to, and we don't market to them.

Can implementation specialists join?

Yes. If you build websites, CRMs, automations or software, or provide regulated services such as legal, tax or cybersecurity, we can refer scoped work to you when a client needs it.
