How it works

How does ordering work?

No sign-up and no payment up front. You see the exact price before you decide.

STEP 1

Send a brief

Pick a service and fill in a short form: what you need, by when, in which language. Share photos or documents by link.

STEP 2

Get your quote

We email the price, delivery time and secure payment options, usually within one working day.

STEP 3

Review and receive

Once you've paid, we make it, send a first version for review, make one round of changes and deliver the final files.

Paying

How can I pay?

Your quote email includes secure payment options for your country. In India: UPI, cards and netbanking through Razorpay, or bank transfer. Elsewhere: cards through Stripe, PayPal, or bank transfer.

FAQ

Questions about Studio

What is MarketCentaur Studio?

Studio is the part of MarketCentaur that makes things for individuals and families: videos, profiles, career documents, channels, study packs and personal AI setups. We use AI tools to work quickly, and a person reviews every piece before it reaches you.

Why don't you show prices on the website?

Every order is a little different: the language, the length, the deadline and the extras all change the work. So you send a short brief and we email an exact quote, with secure payment options, usually within one working day. You pay only if you accept it.

How can I pay?

Customers in India can pay by UPI, cards or netbanking through Razorpay, or by bank transfer. Customers elsewhere can pay by card through Stripe, by PayPal, or by bank transfer. The options are listed in your quote email.

What if I want changes?

Each order includes one round of changes after you see the first version. Bigger changes to the brief after work has started may be quoted separately, and we always tell you before doing extra work.

Do you work with customers outside India?

Yes. We work online with customers anywhere, in English and a growing list of other languages.

Is this for businesses too?

Studio is for personal orders. For business needs, see our services for businesses: websites, AI front desks, content, automation and more.

Not sure which service fits?

Write to us with what you have in mind and we'll suggest the right option and quote it.
