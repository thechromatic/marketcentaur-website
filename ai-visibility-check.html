About the check

How does the free AI visibility check work?

You enter your business name, what you sell and where you sell it. The tool writes five questions a real buyer might ask, opens each one in ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google AI Mode, and you record what the answer said about you. It then scores your visibility and suggests next steps.

Do you store what I type into the check?

No. The check runs in your browser and nothing is sent to us unless you choose to request the emailed snapshot at the end.

Why do answers change when I ask again?

AI assistants generate answers fresh each time and can personalize them, so results vary between sessions, users and model versions. That's why a full audit tests more questions, several times, across more assistants.

What should I do if I score low?

Start with the recommendations shown with your score. If you want help, request the free snapshot or a full AI Search Visibility audit, and we'll show you exactly what to fix first.
