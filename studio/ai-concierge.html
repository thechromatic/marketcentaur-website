FAQ

Questions about AI concierge

Do I need to pay for AI subscriptions?

Not always. Many tasks work on free plans. If a paid plan would help, we'll explain the options and you decide; you pay the provider directly.

Do you need my passwords?

No. We guide you to sign in yourself during the session. We never ask you to send passwords by email or chat.

Is it safe to connect AI to my email?

It can be, with the right settings. We explain what each connection can see, switch on the privacy options available, and leave out anything you're not comfortable with.

Can you help my parents in another city?

Yes. Sessions are online, so we can help family members anywhere, at a time that suits them.
