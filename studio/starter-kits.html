FAQ

Questions about starter kits

Do I need a domain name?

Not to start. We can publish your one-page site on a free address, or set it up on your own domain if you buy one; we'll explain the options in the quote.

Who owns the website and profiles?

You do. Everything is set up in your name and accounts, and we hand over all files and logins.

Can you help me later with more?

Yes. Once you're up and running, you can order more posts, updates or a bigger website at any time.
