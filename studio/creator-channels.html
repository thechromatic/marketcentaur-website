FAQ

Questions about creator channels

Can you guarantee views or income?

No. Growth depends on the topic, consistency, the platform and many things nobody controls. We focus on doing the parts that are in our hands well: research, quality, consistency and clear reporting.

Do you follow platform rules on AI content?

Yes. We follow YouTube and Meta rules, including labelling realistic AI-generated content where the platform requires it, and we use licensed music and footage.

Who owns the channel?

You do. The channel is created in your account and you keep all logins. We work with the access you give us and you can remove it at any time.

Can I pause or stop monthly production?

Yes. Monthly production runs month to month and can be paused or stopped with notice before the next month starts.
