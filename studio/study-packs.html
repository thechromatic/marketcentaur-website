FAQ

Questions about study packs

Can students under 18 order?

Orders for students under 18 must be placed by a parent or guardian, who receives the quote and the materials.

Are the notes accurate?

Notes and questions are prepared with AI tools from the syllabus and checked by a person before delivery. Always use the official syllabus and the school's or exam body's guidance as the final word.

Is this a replacement for a teacher?

No. The pack gives structure and practice alongside teachers, classes and textbooks.

Can you include past-paper practice?

We write practice questions in the style of the exam. We don't copy questions from copyrighted papers or books, but we can point you to official sample papers where they exist.
