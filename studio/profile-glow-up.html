FAQ

Questions about profile glow-up

Will you change how I look in photos?

No. We improve lighting, colour, sharpness and backgrounds so the photos look like you on a good day. We don't change faces or body shape, and we won't make photos that misrepresent you.

Can I order a profile for my son, daughter or sibling?

Yes, as long as they know about it and agree. We write from the details you share and the final text should be checked by them.

Can you write in Indian languages?

Yes. Tell us the language in the brief and we'll confirm in the quote.

Will you write anything untrue?

No. A good profile is honest. We help you present real details warmly and clearly.
