Deliverables

  • Ideal-customer profile (ICP) written up from a kickoff workshop and your best existing customers
  • Ranked target-account list (25 accounts as standard), each with fit score, rationale and source links
  • Buying-role map: the likely decision-makers and influencers for each priority account
