MarketCentaur is developing a practical, staged approach to growth intelligence: connect ongoing market awareness with ideal-customer-fit analysis, account research, campaign preparation and performance learning. The initial service can be delivered with human review and low-code tools; deeper integrations and autonomy are introduced only where they are reliable, useful and appropriate.

01 / MONITOR

Monitor the external environment

Track relevant industry news, competitor moves, customer signals, regulatory changes and company announcements using an agreed source list.

  • Source-linked signal summaries
  • Relevance and confidence notes
  • Weekly or agreed-cadence briefings
02 / IDENTIFY

Find potential demand triggers

Interpret which developments may indicate a customer need, new buying priority, emerging risk or market-entry opportunity.

  • Evidence and rationale
  • Potential business implications
  • Open questions for validation
03 / MATCH

Match opportunities to the ICP

Compare signals against target sectors, company characteristics, use cases, geography and agreed ideal-customer criteria.

  • Fit rationale and prioritization
  • Segment and account shortlist
  • Disqualifying factors where known
04 / RESEARCH

Research accounts and buying roles

Build a concise, sourced picture of target companies and relevant decision-making roles using appropriate public or customer-authorized data.

  • Account context and evidence links
  • Relevant business challenges
  • Likely stakeholders to validate
05 / PLAN

Create campaign recommendations

Translate the evidence into a message angle, value hypothesis, channel recommendation and next-best action tailored to the segment.

  • Campaign brief and messaging
  • Content and offer recommendations
  • Clear assumptions and QA checklist
06 / ACT WITH APPROVAL

Prepare and coordinate execution

Prepare outreach drafts, campaign assets and approved follow-up tasks. Publishing, contact, spend and other consequential actions remain behind agreed review gates.

  • Human approval before sending or publishing
  • Suppression, consent and platform checks
  • Exception handling and audit trail
07 / MEASURE

Measure outcomes that matter

Connect activity to meaningful indicators such as qualified replies, meetings, opportunities, pipeline progression and conversion where data is available.

  • Defined metrics and reporting cadence
  • Campaign and segment comparisons
  • Data-quality limitations made visible
08 / IMPROVE

Refine the next decision

Use observed outcomes and human feedback to adjust account priorities, message hypotheses, source selection and workflow rules.

  • Documented learning and changes
  • Updated priorities and next actions
  • Review of what did not work
