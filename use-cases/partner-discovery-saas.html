Use case · B2B & industrial

Finding channel and integration partners for SaaS companies

For SaaS companies that want growth through resellers, agencies or integrations, not just direct sales.

Short answer

Good partners already serve your ideal customers without competing with you. We agree partner-fit criteria with you, research agencies, resellers, consultancies and technology companies that match, rank them by fit, and suggest a relationship angle and first approach for each. Your team then starts the conversations.

The problem

What usually goes wrong

  • Partner programmes launch with a page but no list of who to approach.
  • Inbound partner requests vary in quality, with no way to compare them.
  • It is unclear which partner types fit the product best.
How it works

How we approach it

01 / STEP

Define fit

The customers you share, the role a partner could play and the regions in scope.

02 / STEP

Research candidates

Agencies, resellers, consultancies and integration partners, from public sources.

03 / STEP

Rank and suggest angles

Each candidate is ranked by fit, with a suggested relationship type.

04 / STEP

Plan first approaches

We walk through the shortlist and agree which conversations to test first.

What you get

What you receive

  • A ranked shortlist of partner candidates
  • A suggested relationship angle for each
  • Agreed first conversations to test

Do you contact the partners?
