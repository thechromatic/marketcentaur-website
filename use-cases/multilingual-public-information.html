Use case · Professional & public

Clear, multilingual public information for public bodies

For councils, agencies and public services that need key information understood by people in several languages.

Short answer

Residents can only act on information they understand. We adapt your most-used service pages and notices into the languages your communities speak, using AI for drafts and reviewers for meaning, tone and terminology. We also check the pages for plain language, so the original is clear before it is translated.

The problem

What usually goes wrong

  • Key service information exists in one language only.
  • Translations are commissioned page by page, with inconsistent terms.
  • Original pages use jargon that does not translate well.
How it works

How we approach it

01 / STEP

Choose the pages

We agree the most-used pages and notices, and the languages to cover.

02 / STEP

Plain-language pass

The source text is simplified first, so translations are clearer.

03 / STEP

Translate and review

AI drafts, then reviewers check meaning, tone and a shared glossary.

04 / STEP

Keep in step

A routine for updating translations when source pages change.

What you get

What you receive

  • Priority pages in the languages you serve
  • A shared glossary of approved terms
  • A routine for keeping translations current
