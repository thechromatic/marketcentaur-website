Use case · B2B & industrial

Getting ready for e-invoicing deadlines in India, the UAE and Saudi Arabia

For finance teams, exporters and groups with entities in markets where e-invoicing becomes mandatory in 2027.

Short answer

Several countries are widening e-invoicing in 2027, and most delays come from invoice data and processes, not software. We list the deadlines that apply to each of your entities, check your invoice data and billing steps against them, and prepare a cut-over and testing plan with your accredited provider or ERP partner.

The problem

What usually goes wrong

  • Deadlines differ by country, entity and revenue, and nobody owns the full list.
  • Customer, tax and product data on invoices is incomplete or inconsistent.
  • Testing starts too late, so the first live invoices are rejected.
How it works

How we approach it

01 / STEP

List the deadlines

Every e-invoicing date that applies to each of your entities, with sources.

02 / STEP

Check the data

Invoice fields, customer records and tax codes compared with what each system will need.

03 / STEP

Fix the process

Billing steps, approvals and exception handling updated for the new flow.

04 / STEP

Plan cut-over

A testing and go-live checklist agreed with your provider or ERP partner.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are you an accredited e-invoicing provider?

No. We prepare your processes, data and testing, and work alongside the accredited provider or ERP partner you choose.

Does this replace our tax adviser?

No. Your tax adviser confirms which rules apply. We turn those rules into clean data, clear steps and a tested cut-over.

Can you automate invoice handling at the same time?

Yes. Finance Ops Automation can automate a defined invoice or payables workflow, with human approvals, once the e-invoicing basics are in place.
