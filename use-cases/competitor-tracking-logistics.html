Use case · B2B & industrial

Tracking competitor moves and new lanes for logistics companies

For freight forwarders, 3PLs and carriers that learn about competitor moves from customers.

Short answer

Competitors announce new lanes, warehouses, partnerships and pricing changes in public long before customers mention them. We agree which competitors and topics matter, monitor public sources, and send a regular brief of what changed, what it likely means for you and what to do next. Observations and interpretation are always labelled separately.

The problem

What usually goes wrong

  • Competitor moves are discovered late, often from a customer.
  • Nobody owns tracking competitors, so it happens in bursts before big pitches.
  • Market news is easy to find but hard to turn into decisions.
How it works

How we approach it

01 / STEP

Agree the watchlist

Competitors, lanes, regions and topics that affect your business.

02 / STEP

Monitor public sources

Company announcements, job listings, filings, pricing pages and trade coverage.

03 / STEP

Review and interpret

A person checks each item and notes what it likely means for you.

04 / STEP

Brief your team

A regular brief with recommended actions, plus a deeper brief when needed.

What you get

What you receive

  • A regular brief on competitor and market moves
  • Each item sourced and dated
  • Recommended actions, labelled as recommendations

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is this legal and ethical?

Yes. We use public, verifiable sources only and never seek confidential information.

How is this different from a news alert?

Alerts deliver everything. We filter for relevance, check facts, and say what an item likely means for you.

Can we get a one-off deep dive?

Yes. A market and competitor brief can be scoped as a single project, with ongoing monitoring afterwards if useful.
