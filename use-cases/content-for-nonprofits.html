Use case · Professional & public

A steady flow of content for nonprofits with small teams

For nonprofits that have strong stories to tell and too little time to tell them.

Short answer

Small teams struggle to post, email and report regularly. We turn one interview or a few documents each month into short videos, posts, a newsletter and an article in your voice. A person checks every fact and you approve everything before it goes out. We also review your donation and volunteer pages, so new interest turns into action.

The problem

What usually goes wrong

  • Content stops whenever the team is busy with programme work.
  • Impact stories exist but are never turned into posts or newsletters.
  • Donation and volunteer pages ask for too much, too early.
How it works

How we approach it

01 / STEP

Monthly brief

One interview or a few documents set the month's stories and messages.

02 / STEP

Produce and review

Videos, posts, a newsletter and an article are drafted with AI and checked by a person.

03 / STEP

You approve

Nothing goes out without your approval.

04 / STEP

Improve sign-up pages

We review donation and volunteer pages and rank fixes.

What you get

What you receive

  • A monthly set of content in your voice
  • Facts checked before you see a draft
  • A ranked fix list for donation and volunteer pages
