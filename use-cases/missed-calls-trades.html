Use case · Local & consumer

Booking more jobs from the calls trades businesses miss

For plumbers, electricians, heating engineers and other field-service firms whose teams are on site when the phone rings.

Short answer

When engineers are on a job, calls go to voicemail and many callers try the next company. An AI front desk answers calls and messages, takes the job details and postcode, offers available slots and logs each lead for your office. Emergencies go straight to a person. We set it up on your services, areas and booking rules.

The problem

What usually goes wrong

  • Engineers can not answer the phone while working, and voicemails are often not returned the same day.
  • Website forms ask for too much, so mobile visitors give up before sending.
  • Leads arrive by phone, text and email with no single list of who is waiting.
How it works

How we approach it

01 / STEP

Review the journey

We check how a customer reaches you today, from search result to call or form.

02 / STEP

Set up the front desk

An AI front desk answers calls and messages with your services, areas and call-out rules.

03 / STEP

Shorten the form

We cut the website form to the details your office actually uses.

04 / STEP

Log every lead

Each enquiry lands in one list or CRM, with emergencies flagged for a call back.

What you get

What you receive

  • Calls and messages answered while the team is on site
  • A shorter, mobile-friendly booking form
  • One list of every enquiry, with emergencies flagged

Frequently asked questions

What happens with emergency calls?

You decide what counts as an emergency. Those calls are passed to a person straight away, by transfer or an immediate alert, depending on your setup.

Do customers know it is automated?

Yes. The assistant introduces itself as an automated assistant and offers a call back from the team when a caller prefers it.
