Use case · Local & consumer

Consistent local search and AI visibility for multi-location businesses

For chains, franchises and groups whose locations show different details in search, maps and AI answers.

Short answer

Customers find each location through maps, search and AI assistants, and they all read the same public facts. We audit every location for consistent names, hours, services and reviews, fix the gaps, give each location a page that answers local questions, and check how assistants describe you. Updates then follow one agreed process.

The problem

What usually goes wrong

  • Opening hours, phone numbers and services differ between listings, the website and AI answers.
  • Location pages are thin copies of each other, so they rarely appear for local searches.
  • Reviews are answered at some locations and ignored at others.
How it works

How we approach it

01 / STEP

Audit every location

We compare listings, location pages and AI answers for each site, and score the gaps.

02 / STEP

Fix the facts

Names, hours, services and contact details are made consistent everywhere they appear.

03 / STEP

Improve location pages

Each location gets a page that answers the questions local customers ask.

04 / STEP

Set an update routine

One process for holiday hours, closures and new services, so details stay in step.

What you get

What you receive

  • A location-by-location visibility scorecard
  • Consistent details across listings, website and AI answers
  • A simple routine for keeping every location up to date

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee map or AI rankings?

No one can. We fix the factors you control, such as accurate details, useful location pages and review responses, and measure where you stand over time.

How many locations can you cover?

Scope is set by the number of locations and listings. It is agreed in writing after the scoping call.

Who keeps the details updated afterwards?

Your team, using the update routine we set up, or us through ongoing support if you prefer.
