Use case · Local & consumer

Turning course enquiries into enrolments for training providers

For training providers, academies and colleges whose enquiries arrive faster than the team can answer them.

Short answer

Prospective students ask the same questions about dates, fees, entry requirements and formats, often outside office hours. An AI assistant answers those from your approved course information, collects details and books calls with your advisers. We also review the course pages, so the next step is clear before anyone needs to ask.

The problem

What usually goes wrong

  • Enquiries peak in the evenings and at weekends, when no adviser is available.
  • Course pages bury dates, formats and entry requirements.
  • Follow-up depends on whoever picks up the inbox that day.
How it works

How we approach it

01 / STEP

Collect the answers

We gather approved answers on dates, formats, requirements and policies.

02 / STEP

Set up the assistant

It answers routine questions on chat or WhatsApp and books calls with advisers.

03 / STEP

Improve course pages

Key facts move to the top of each course page, with one clear next step.

04 / STEP

Review monthly

We read a sample of conversations and fill gaps in the answers.

What you get

What you receive

  • Routine course questions answered at any hour
  • Calls booked with advisers, with enquiry details attached
  • Clearer course pages with one next step

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can it answer questions about fees?

It can share the fee information you publish and approve. Questions about individual circumstances, such as funding or discounts, go to an adviser.

Will students know it is automated?

Yes. It introduces itself as an automated assistant and offers to book a call with a person.

Can it work in more than one language?

Yes, if your approved answers are available in those languages. We can help translate them with human review.
