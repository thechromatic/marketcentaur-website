# Website conversion checklist
MarketCentaur free toolkit · marketcentaur.com/toolkit

Mark each item Yes / No / Unsure. Fix the "No" items in the Message and Offer sections first: they usually matter most.

## 1. Message clarity
- [ ] A first-time visitor can say what you do, for whom, within five seconds of landing
- [ ] The headline names an outcome, not a category ("Get paid 2x faster", not "Invoicing software")
- [ ] The subheading says who it is for
- [ ] Industry jargon is explained or removed
- [ ] Each key page has one main idea
- [ ] Copy uses the words your customers use (check reviews and sales calls)

## 2. Proof
- [ ] Proof sits near the top of the page: logos, a number, a short testimonial or a sample
- [ ] Testimonials name a specific result
- [ ] At least one case study or worked example is one click away
- [ ] Claims are specific and checkable
- [ ] Your process is visible: what happens after someone gets in touch
- [ ] Contact details and company information are easy to find

## 3. Offer
- [ ] There is one primary call to action per page
- [ ] The call to action says what happens next ("Book a 20-minute call", not "Submit")
- [ ] A lower-commitment option exists for visitors who are not ready (a free check, a template, a sample)
- [ ] It is clear what the visitor gets and how long it takes
- [ ] Objections are answered (an FAQ near the call to action)
- [ ] Risk reducers are stated (no obligation, fixed quote, cancel any time)

## 4. Friction
- [ ] Pages load in under 3 seconds on mobile (test with PageSpeed Insights)
- [ ] Forms ask only for what you need now (email plus one or two fields)
- [ ] Buttons and links are easy to tap on mobile
- [ ] No pop-up covers the content in the first 10 seconds
- [ ] Navigation has seven items or fewer
- [ ] Every page has a clear next step at the bottom

## 5. Follow-up
- [ ] Every form sends an instant confirmation saying when you will reply
- [ ] Someone replies within one business day
- [ ] Leads are logged in one place (CRM or spreadsheet) with their source
- [ ] People who download something get one useful follow-up
- [ ] You can see which pages and sources produce enquiries (analytics goals set up)
- [ ] You review the enquiry numbers at least once a month
