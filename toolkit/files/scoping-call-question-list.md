# Scoping call question list
MarketCentaur free toolkit · marketcentaur.com/toolkit.html

The questions we ask before quoting any project. Use them to scope your own work, or answer them before you brief any agency or freelancer.

## The goal
1. What outcome do you want in the next 90 days? How will you know it worked?
2. Why now? What changed that makes this a priority?
3. What happens if you do nothing for another six months?

## The current state
4. What have you already tried? What worked and what didn't?
5. Which numbers do you track today (traffic, leads, conversion, pipeline, time spent)?
6. Which tools and systems are involved (website platform, CRM, analytics, email)?
7. Where does the data live, and who has access to it?

## The customer
8. Who is your best customer, and why did they buy?
9. Who is a bad-fit customer you'd rather not attract?
10. Which competitors come up most in sales conversations?

## Scope and constraints
11. What is definitely in scope? What is definitely out?
12. What deadline is fixed, and what is flexible?
13. Who approves the work, and who needs to be consulted?
14. Are there rules to respect (brand, legal, data privacy, industry regulation)?

## Delivery
15. What format do you need the output in (report, spreadsheet, live changes, training)?
16. Who will act on the output, and how much time do they have?
17. How should progress be reported, and how often?
18. What would make you say this project was worth it?
