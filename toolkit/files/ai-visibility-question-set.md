## Discovery questions (buyer doesn't know you yet)
1. What are the best [service] providers in [city or region]?
2. Who should I hire for [service] for a [industry] business?
3. What is the best [service] for a small business?
4. Which [service] companies work with [industry] clients?
5. Recommend an affordable [service] provider.
6. What are alternatives to [competitor]?
7. Top-rated [service] agencies for mid-sized companies.
8. Who offers [service] with a fixed price?

## Problem questions (buyer knows the problem, not the solution)
9. How do I fix [the problem you solve]?
10. Why is my [problem symptom, e.g. website not getting enquiries]?
11. Should I hire someone or do [service] myself?
12. What does [service] usually include?
13. How long does [service] take?
14. What should I ask before hiring a [service] provider?
15. What mistakes do companies make with [service]?

## Comparison questions (buyer is shortlisting)
16. [Your brand] vs [competitor]: which is better?
17. Is [your brand] any good?
18. What do customers say about [your brand]?
19. What does [your brand] do?
20. Who are [your brand]'s competitors?
21. Is [your brand] a good fit for a [industry] company?

## Decision questions (buyer is ready)
22. How do I contact [your brand]?
23. Does [your brand] work with clients in [country]?
24. What is [your brand]'s process?
25. Can [your brand] help with [specific deliverable]?

## Scoring
- Named in 0 to 5 answers: AI assistants don't know you yet. Start with consistent descriptions on your site, directories and profiles.
- Named in 6 to 15: you're known, but not for the right questions. Build pages that answer the discovery and problem questions directly.
- Named in 16+: protect your position. Re-test monthly and correct any inaccurate descriptions.
