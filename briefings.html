How often do Briefings arrive?

Once a month per sector. You can choose more than one sector.

Are Briefings free?

Yes. Briefings are free. If you need signals tracked for your own competitors and accounts every week, that is our Weekly Signal Watch service.

Where does the information come from?

Public sources such as company announcements, filings, job posts, regulator updates and trade press. Every signal links to its source, and anything that is our opinion is labelled as opinion.

Can I unsubscribe?

Yes, from the link in any issue or by emailing hello@marketcentaur.com.
