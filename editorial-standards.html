Editorial standards

How we research, write and check our work.

These standards apply to everything MarketCentaur publishes, including Insights articles, sample briefs and industry pages, and to the deliverables we send clients. Articles are published by the MarketCentaur Team.

How do we use AI?

AI helps us research faster: gathering public sources, summarising long documents, drafting outlines and checking for gaps. It does not decide what is true or what we recommend. A person reviews every piece before it is published or delivered, checks claims against their sources, and is accountable for the final wording.

How do we choose and cite sources?

  • Primary sources first: company websites, filings, official statistics, product documentation and original research, before commentary or aggregators.
  • Every important claim is traceable: client deliverables link each finding to its source. Articles link to sources where a specific fact or figure is used.
  • Facts and interpretation are separated: we say clearly when something is our judgment rather than an established fact.
  • No invented data: we don't publish statistics, quotes, testimonials or case studies that can't be verified.

How is content reviewed?

Each article is written to answer a real question buyers ask, with a short answer at the top and practical steps below. Before publishing, a reviewer checks accuracy, sources, clarity and whether the advice is safe to follow. Articles show their publication date, and the date they were last updated when they change materially.

Independence and commercial interests

MarketCentaur sells the services some articles describe. Where an article mentions our own services, it says so. We don't accept payment for coverage, and we don't rank or recommend tools or vendors in exchange for fees.

How do we label samples and illustrations?

Sample deliverables and the illustrative screens on this site use fictional companies and are labelled as samples or illustrations. They show the format and depth of our work, not results for a real client. When we publish a case study or testimonial, it will be a real one, shared with the client's written permission.

How do we handle corrections?

If you spot an error, email hello@marketcentaur.com. We review every report, correct confirmed errors promptly, and update the article's modified date.
