Flagship system

Growth Intelligence System: from market signal to approved action

Our long-term goal is one connected loop that watches your market, spots opportunities, researches accounts, prepares campaigns and learns from the results. We start each client with a human-reviewed pilot on one market and segment, then automate more steps as they prove reliable.

01 · MONITOR

Watch the market

Industry news, competitor moves, customer signals and regulatory changes from an agreed source list.

02 · IDENTIFY

Spot demand triggers

Flag developments that suggest a new need, buying priority or market opening.

03 · MATCH

Match to your ICP

Score opportunities against your target sectors, company profile, use cases and geography.

04 · RESEARCH

Research accounts

Build sourced account profiles and identify the likely buying roles.

05 · PLAN

Recommend campaigns

Message angle, value hypothesis, channel and next-best action for each segment.

06 · ACT

Execute with approval

Draft outreach and assets. Nothing is sent, published or paid for without human sign-off.

07 · MEASURE

Track what matters

Qualified replies, meetings, opportunities and pipeline progress.

08 · IMPROVE

Refine

Adjust priorities, messages and sources based on what actually worked.
