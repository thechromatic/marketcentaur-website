Ground rules

How we work with agencies

Unbranded

Deliverables carry your branding or none. Our name does not appear unless you want it to.

NDA first

We sign your NDA before receiving any client information.

Your clients stay yours

We never contact your clients unless you ask us to, and we never market to them.

You set the price

You price the work to your clients. Our quote to you is fixed per order after scoping.

Human-reviewed

A person checks every deliverable before it reaches you, and observations are kept separate from opinion.

Clear scope

Each order has a written scope and turnaround, confirmed before work starts.

How ordering works

From brief to delivery

  • Scoping call. We agree which deliverables you need, your templates and how you want to receive work.
  • NDA and brief. We sign your NDA, then you send the client brief through the channel you choose.
  • Written scope. Each order gets a scope, a turnaround and a fixed quote before work begins.
  • Delivery and review. A person reviews the work before it reaches you, and we answer your questions before you present it.
Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do we start?

Book a scoping call, then send one real project. A first order is the simplest way to judge the quality and format before committing to more.

Can deliverables use our templates?

Yes. We can work in your report template and style guide, or deliver in a clean unbranded format you can restyle.

What happens if a turnaround slips?

Turnarounds are confirmed in each order's scope. If anything changes, you hear from us before the deadline, not after it.

Do you offer video?

Not on the white-label desk at the moment. Written, research and localization work is available now.
