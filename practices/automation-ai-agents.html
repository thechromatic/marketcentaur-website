Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between automation and an AI agent?

An automation follows defined steps. An agent can choose between permitted actions to complete a task, so its scope and safeguards need careful design.

Which platforms can you work with?

We assess your existing systems and recommend an approach that fits your environment rather than requiring a particular platform.

Can an agent act without a person reviewing it?

The level of autonomy is agreed for each use case. We design approval or escalation points for actions that need human judgement.
