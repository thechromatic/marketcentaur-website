Will the assistant replace our customer service team?

It handles agreed routine interactions and supports your team, with clear routes to a person when a question needs judgement.

How do you keep answers accurate?

We use approved information sources, test representative questions and define what the assistant does when it cannot answer confidently.

Can it connect to our booking or support tools?

We assess your systems during scoping and confirm what is practical for the chosen use case.
