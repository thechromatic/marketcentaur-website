Frequently asked questions

Is this legal advice?

No. This is readiness and governance support, not legal advice. Consult qualified counsel for legal interpretation.

Do you certify compliance?

No. We document practices, identify readiness gaps and plan actions; we do not certify compliance.

Which EU AI Act dates matter now?

AI-literacy duties already apply, Article 50 transparency rules took effect on 2 August 2026, and stand-alone high-risk obligations start on 2 December 2027.
