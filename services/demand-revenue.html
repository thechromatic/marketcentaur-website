How we can help

We shape the scope around your goals, audience, existing resources and the channels that make sense for your business. Services can be delivered as a focused project or ongoing support.

01 / SERVICE

B2B lead generation and ABM

Define target accounts, map relevant roles, research buying signals and plan personalized outreach.

02 / SERVICE

Email and lifecycle campaigns

Develop permission-conscious nurture sequences, newsletters and customer re-engagement campaigns.

03 / SERVICE

Performance marketing and paid advertising

Plan paid search, paid social, retargeting, audience tests and creative experiments around qualified leads or sales. Advertising spend is separate.

04 / SERVICE

Lifecycle, retention and loyalty campaigns

Map onboarding, post-purchase, win-back and re-engagement journeys to improve customer experience and repeat business.

05 / SERVICE

Affiliate, referral and partner marketing

Identify complementary partners, shape referral or affiliate program mechanics, and plan co-marketing and partner enablement.

06 / SERVICE

Sales enablement and revenue operations

Align marketing and sales with clearer qualification, lead handoffs, sales playbooks, CRM workflow reviews and pipeline definitions.

07 / SERVICE

Field, event and experiential marketing

Plan trade-show promotion, local activations, sampling, QR-led lead capture and post-event follow-up.

08 / SERVICE

Influencer and partnership campaign planning

Research potential creators or partners and structure campaign briefs, measurement and collaboration plans.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is advertising spend included?

No. Advertising spend is paid directly to the platforms and is separate from our work.

How do you approach outreach?

Permission-conscious and targeted: we plan outreach around researched target accounts and opted-in audiences, with messages that reflect what each segment cares about.

Do you work with B2B and consumer brands?

Yes. Account-based outreach and sales enablement for B2B, and lifecycle, retention and referral campaigns for consumer brands.

Where should a B2B team start?

The B2B Account Intelligence Sprint produces a ranked target-account list, a buying-role map and outreach angles.
