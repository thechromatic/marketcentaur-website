How engagements work

01 / DEFINE

Agree the decision or process

Clarify the goal, available evidence, stakeholders, access needs, exclusions and acceptance criteria.

02 / DELIVER

Produce useful outputs

Deliver the agreed research, report, scorecard, process map or workflow plan with sources and limitations.

03 / IMPROVE

Review what should happen next

Where ongoing value exists, agree a recurring reporting, monitoring or improvement cadence.

Engagements are tailored to your business goals. Formal legal, tax, financial, clinical, cybersecurity assurance, environmental certification, audit and regulated compliance advice must be provided by appropriately qualified professionals. Supplier research can support, but does not replace, formal due diligence. AI and data workflows include appropriate privacy, security, accuracy and human-review controls.
