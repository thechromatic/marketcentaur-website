A clear, scoped process

STEP 01

Understand the work

Agree the problem, people involved, existing process and evidence available.

STEP 02

Prioritize improvements

Compare potential value, effort, dependencies and risks before recommending changes.

STEP 03

Document and review

Provide agreed deliverables, define ownership and identify how usefulness should be assessed.

Scope, implementation responsibility, access requirements and delivery capacity are confirmed for each engagement. Financial, legal, tax, clinical, cybersecurity assurance and regulated compliance advice or certification must be provided by appropriately qualified professionals. Automation and AI-enabled workflows should include suitable privacy, security, accuracy and human-review controls.
