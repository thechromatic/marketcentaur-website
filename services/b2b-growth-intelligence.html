How the engagement works

A straightforward process keeps the work focused, verifiable and useful.

1. Scope

Agree on the ICP, geography, account volume or market question, timeframe and intended use.

2. Research

Review relevant company, market and role information against the agreed research scope.

3. Review

Check material findings, add source notes, and label analysis or hypotheses clearly.

4. Deliver

Receive a structured report or recurring digest with relevant context and suggested next actions.

A note on signals and data: hiring, expansion, leadership, product and partnership developments can be useful research signals, but they do not prove a company is ready to buy. Our services focus on research and analysis rather than mass outreach. Research is reviewed before delivery, and scope is agreed up front.
