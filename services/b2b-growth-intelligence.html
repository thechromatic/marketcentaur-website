Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which service should we start with?

If the question is who to target, start with account and persona mapping. If it is what is changing around you, start with market and competitor intelligence. If growth depends on relationships, start with partner discovery. If you need to stay current, choose the Weekly Growth Signal Watch.

Can we combine services?

Yes. A common sequence is account mapping first, then the Weekly Growth Signal Watch on the accounts that matter most.

Where does the information come from?

Public, verifiable sources. Every key finding is cited with its date, and observations are kept separate from our analysis.

Is there an example of the output?

Yes. Read the sample intelligence brief to see the structure, sourcing and next actions.

Not sure which service fits?

Tell us your market, audience and the decision you're trying to make. We'll help define a practical starting scope.
