Why it matters

Most lost leads aren't rejected. They just weren't answered.

Small teams can't pick up every call, especially during jobs, appointments, evenings and weekends. Customers who reach voicemail or wait hours for a reply often contact the next business on the list.

  • Calls go to voicemail during busy hours or after closing
  • Staff repeat the same answers about prices, hours and availability
  • WhatsApp and website chat messages wait hours for a reply
  • Inquiries are logged inconsistently, or not at all
  • You want 24/7 coverage without hiring for night shifts

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Will callers know they're speaking to an AI?

Yes. We recommend clear disclosure at the start of each conversation, and we follow the disclosure and call-recording rules that apply in your country. Callers can ask for a person at any time.

What happens when the AI can't answer?

It follows the handoff rules you approve: transferring the call, taking a detailed message, or creating a callback task for your team. Emergencies, complaints and sensitive topics are always handed to a person.

Which languages are supported?

Most major languages, including English, Hindi, Spanish, Arabic, French and German, plus many regional languages. We test each language with sample conversations before launch.

Can it connect to our calendar and CRM?

Usually, yes. Common calendars, booking tools and CRMs such as Google Calendar, Outlook, Calendly, HubSpot and Zoho can be connected directly or through automation tools.

Is it suitable for medical or legal questions?

The assistant handles administrative inquiries only, such as bookings, opening hours and general service information. It does not give medical, legal or financial advice, and those questions are passed to qualified staff.
