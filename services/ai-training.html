Why it matters

Access to AI isn't the same as getting value from it.

Licenses are easy to buy. The hard part is changing everyday habits: knowing which tasks to hand to AI, how to check the output, and what data must never be pasted into a tool.

  • AI tool usage is low or limited to a few enthusiasts
  • People aren't sure what's safe to share with AI tools
  • Output quality varies widely across the team
  • Leaders want a realistic view of AI opportunities and risks
  • You need a simple AI-use policy people actually follow

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which AI tools do you cover?

We teach on the tools your team already has, most often ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini and Claude, plus common automation tools. The skills transfer across tools.

Do people need technical skills?

No. Workshops are designed for non-technical staff. More advanced sessions on automation and agents are available for teams that want them.

Can you train teams outside India?

Yes. We deliver online workshops worldwide and can schedule across time zones.

Will we get materials to keep?

Yes. Every workshop includes a prompt library, quick-reference guides and exercises your team can reuse.

Can the training include our AI-use policy?

Yes. We can draft a practical, one-page AI-use policy with you and teach it as part of the program.
