Why it matters

Translation gets you understood. Localization gets you chosen.

Buyers trust content in their own language and with local examples, formats and references. Direct translation often misses tone, terms and the details that matter locally.

  • You're entering a new country or region
  • Distributors and partners need material in their language
  • Training or onboarding content only exists in one language
  • Videos would reach more people with subtitles or voiceover
  • Translations are inconsistent across teams and vendors

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is AI translation accurate enough for business use?

For most marketing, sales and training content, AI produces a strong first draft. The quality comes from the review: we check meaning, tone and terminology, adapt local references, and use native-speaker reviewers for languages where you need publication-grade quality.

Which languages do you cover?

All major world languages and many regional ones, including Indian languages. We'll confirm the review approach for each language during scoping.

Can you handle legal or medical documents?

We focus on marketing, sales, training and product content. Regulated documents such as contracts or medical instructions need certified translators, and we can help you arrange that through partners.

What file formats can you work with?

Word, PowerPoint, PDF, InDesign exports, spreadsheets, subtitle files (SRT/VTT), web page exports and most course formats. We return files in the same format wherever possible.

How do you keep terms consistent?

We build a glossary and style guide for each language at the start and use it on every piece, so product names and key terms stay consistent across your materials.

Ready to reach a new market?

Tell us the languages and materials you have in mind, and we'll reply with a suggested scope and the review approach for each language.
