We separate observed developments from analysis so your team can identify the most relevant next steps.

How it works

From question to brief

01 / STEP

Scope

Agree the market, competitors and questions that matter, and the decision the brief should inform.

02 / STEP

Research

Review public sources such as competitor websites, pricing pages, job posts, announcements and industry coverage.

03 / STEP

Review

A person checks every key finding, records its source and date, and keeps observation separate from analysis.

04 / STEP

Walkthrough

We take you through the implications and the watchlist, and agree the follow-up questions worth pursuing.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What sources do you use?

Public, verifiable sources: company websites and pricing pages, press releases, job listings, filings where available, reviews and industry publications. Each key finding carries its source and date.

How is this different from the Weekly Growth Signal Watch?

This brief is a one-off, deeper look at a market or a set of competitors. The Weekly Growth Signal Watch is a short recurring update on an agreed watchlist. Many teams start with a brief, then move to monitoring.

How long does it take?

The timeline is set in your written scope after the free scoping call. It depends on how many competitors and questions the brief covers.

Can we see an example first?

Yes. The sample intelligence brief shows the format: sourced findings, implications and recommended next actions.
