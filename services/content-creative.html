Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you use AI to create content?

AI helps with drafts, repurposing and variations. A person shapes the ideas, checks the facts and approves everything before it reaches you.

What do you need from us?

Approved source material such as product information, past content, recordings or an interview, plus one person who can approve drafts.

Is this a project or ongoing?

Either. Start with a project such as a brand voice guide or a campaign, or get a monthly flow of content through the AI Content & Video Studio.

Can you work alongside our designers or agency?

Yes. We can supply briefs, copy and drafts that follow your existing brand guidelines.
