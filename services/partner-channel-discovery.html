Partner & channel discovery

Find partners that can extend your reach.

Identify potentially complementary organizations and the partnership roles worth exploring, based on your ideal customer, offer and route-to-market priorities.

What you'll receive

A prioritized partner shortlist

  • Partner-fit criteria and target partner types
  • Shortlist of relevant organizations with rationale
  • Likely partnership roles to research
  • Potential relationship angles, such as referrals, resale or co-marketing
When it's useful

When growth depends on relationships

Useful for entering a new geography, building a partner ecosystem, identifying complementary service providers, or testing referral and reseller opportunities.

We help you assess potential fit and plan a relevant first approach to promising partners.

How it works

From fit criteria to first conversations

01 / STEP

Define fit

Agree partner-fit criteria: the customers you share, the role a partner could play and the regions in scope.

02 / STEP

Research

Identify organizations that serve your ideal customer without competing with you, using public sources.

03 / STEP

Prioritize

Rank the shortlist by fit and suggest a relationship angle for each, such as referrals, resale or co-marketing.

04 / STEP

Plan the approach

Walk through the shortlist together and agree which first conversations to test.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of partners can you find?

Referral partners, resellers and distributors, technology and integration partners, agencies and consultancies, and associations that already reach your audience.

Do you contact partners on our behalf?

The deliverable is a researched shortlist with a suggested first approach for each organization. Your team makes the introductions, so each relationship starts with you.

Can this cover a new country or region?

Yes. Tell us the region you are entering and we set the fit criteria and research scope for that market.

How does this fit with your other services?

It pairs well with account and persona mapping, and it is available as an add-on to the B2B Account Intelligence Sprint.

Build a more focused partner pipeline.

Share your offer and the kind of partners you'd like to find. We'll define the first research scope together.
