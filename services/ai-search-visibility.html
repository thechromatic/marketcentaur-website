Why it matters

If AI assistants don't know you, buyers won't either.

AI answers compress a page of search results into a short list of names. Being on that list depends on how clearly your site explains what you do, how consistently other trusted sources describe you, and whether your content answers the questions buyers actually ask.

Most businesses have never checked. The ones that do usually find missing or outdated facts, competitors named in their place, and pages that AI systems can't easily read or cite.

  • AI assistants name competitors, but not you, for your core services
  • Answers describe your business with old or wrong details
  • Your best pages aren't cited as sources
  • Your SEO is fine, but AI referral traffic is close to zero
  • You don't know which buyer questions matter most

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is GEO (generative engine optimization)?

GEO is the practice of improving how AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews understand, describe and recommend your business. It builds on SEO but focuses on clear facts, answer-ready content, structured data and consistent third-party sources rather than rankings alone.

Is GEO different from SEO?

They overlap. SEO helps search engines rank your pages; GEO helps AI systems extract accurate facts about you and cite you in generated answers. Good SEO is a strong foundation, and we work alongside your existing SEO provider if you have one.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend us?

No. AI answers vary by question, user and model version, and nobody controls them. What we can do is measure your visibility consistently, remove the gaps that make you hard to recommend, and track whether mentions and accuracy improve over time.

Which AI assistants do you test?

ChatGPT, Google Gemini and AI Overviews, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot and Claude as standard. We can add regional or industry-specific assistants on request.

How long until we see changes?

Fixes to your own site can be picked up within weeks for assistants that browse the web. Changes that depend on third-party sources or model updates usually take longer, which is why we re-test monthly.
