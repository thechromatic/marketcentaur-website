Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee rankings or AI mentions?

No one can. We improve the factors you control: clear content, sound technical foundations, structured information and consistent public facts about your business.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO is ranking in search results. AEO is being the direct answer in answer boxes and voice results. GEO is being described and recommended by AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Gemini. All three reward clear, accurate, well-structured content.

Do you build websites?

We plan structure, messaging and conversion paths, and either implement agreed changes or coordinate them with your developer.
