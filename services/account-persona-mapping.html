What's included

Every account map is built from the same parts. We adjust the depth and number of accounts to your market, your team and how you plan to use the output.

01

ICP and fit criteria

We write down what a good-fit company looks like, using your best current customers, your target regions and your offer. You approve the criteria before account research begins.

02

Prioritized account shortlist

Target companies are ranked against the agreed criteria. Each one comes with a short explanation of why it's on the list and links to the sources behind it.

03

Buying-role map

For priority accounts, we identify the roles most likely to decide on, influence or use what you sell, so outreach doesn't rest on a single contact.

04

Account context

Concise notes on what each company does and any relevant public developments, such as expansion, hiring or leadership changes, with the date and source.

05

Suggested engagement angles

A practical starting point for each priority account or segment: which problem to raise and why it may matter to that company now. Your team decides what to send.

06

A format your team can use

A working spreadsheet and a short written summary, followed by a walkthrough call. Your team can sort, challenge and update the list as it learns from real conversations.

How it works

Three stages take you from agreed criteria to a list your team can act on. The timeline is confirmed before work starts.

01 · Define

Agree what fit means

We review your offer, your best customers and the markets you want to reach, then agree the fit criteria, the number of accounts and how your team will use the output.

02 · Research

Build and rank the list

We research companies from public sources, score them against the criteria, then map the likely buying roles and add context for the accounts that rank highest.

03 · Review

Check it and hand it over

A person reviews the findings before they reach you. We then walk your team through the list, including the reasoning and the accounts worth questioning.

Frequently asked questions

What is account and persona mapping?

It is a researched view of which companies are worth your team's time and which roles inside them usually influence a purchase. Each account comes with the reasoning behind its place on the list, so your team can check it and disagree with it.

How many accounts will we receive?

It depends on your market and how you plan to use the list, and we agree the number before work starts. The B2B Account Intelligence Sprint includes 25 ranked accounts as standard, and larger lists or additional regions can be added.

How is this different from the B2B Account Intelligence Sprint?

Account mapping focuses on target accounts and buying roles, with a scope sized to your needs. The sprint is a fixed package that also includes buying signals, a competitor snapshot and outreach angles by segment. On a scoping call, we'll tell you which fits better.

How do you use AI, and who checks the work?

We use AI tools to speed up research and drafting. A person reviews the findings before they reach you, and the reasoning for each account links back to its sources.

Can you guarantee this will produce meetings or sales?

No. Results depend on your offer, market and follow-up. We commit to an agreed scope, specific deliverables and a timeline, all in writing before work starts.
