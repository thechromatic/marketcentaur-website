Privacy Policy

How we handle personal data when you visit marketcentaur.com, use our free tools or contact us.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

In short: We collect only what you send us and the technical data needed to run the site. We use it to reply to you and deliver what you ask for. We don’t sell it or use advertising trackers. Free-tool answers stay in your browser unless you ask us to email you results. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

MarketCentaur (“MarketCentaur”, “we”, “us”) is an AI-led transformation consultancy operated from India and serving businesses worldwide. We decide why and how the personal data described in this policy is used, which makes us the “Data Fiduciary” under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the “controller” under the EU and UK GDPR where those laws apply.

This policy covers marketcentaur.com, our free tools, and messages you send us. Personal data we handle while delivering paid work for a client is also governed by the written agreement for that engagement. Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@marketcentaur.com.

2. Information we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us, plus the technical data needed to run the website.

  • Contact form: your name, email address, company name, company website, the service you’re interested in, and your message.
  • Free website audit: your website address and email address.
  • Growth checks and the website inquiry check: your answers are scored in your browser and are not sent to us. If you ask for your results by email, we receive your email address, website, and your answers and score.
  • Free AI Visibility Check: the details you type in (business name, category, location, competitor and website) and the results you record stay in your browser. If you ask for the free snapshot, we receive those details, your recorded results and score, and your email address.
  • Emails and calls: whatever you include when you write to us or speak with us, such as your contact details, job title and information about your business.
  • Technical data: when you visit the site, our hosting and security provider processes your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you request, and similar log data to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Please don’t send us sensitive personal data, such as health, financial account or government ID details, through the website. Our services are designed for business information.

3. How we use information

  • To reply to your inquiry and suggest a starting point.
  • To prepare and send the free reports, audits or snapshots you request.
  • To send one follow-up message about your inquiry or report. You can tell us to stop at any time.
  • To prepare proposals and deliver services you’ve agreed to buy.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the website.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations and to protect our legal rights.

We use your information on the basis of the consent you give when you submit a form or contact us. Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, we also rely on our legitimate interest in answering business inquiries and running our business, and on contract where you become a client. We don’t make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects.

We won’t add you to a newsletter or marketing list unless you ask to join one.

4. How we use AI tools

We use AI tools, including large language model services, to help research, analyse and draft our responses, reports and deliverables. A person reviews this work before it reaches you.

When information you’ve sent us is processed with an AI tool, we use business accounts or settings that don’t allow the provider to use it to train their models wherever those options are available. We share only what is needed for the task, and you can ask us not to use AI tools on your information.

5. Service providers and third-party services

We use trusted providers to run the website and our business. They process data on our behalf and only for the services they provide:

  • Website hosting and security: Cloudflare.
  • Website analytics: Google Analytics and Cloudflare Web Analytics, as described in section 11.
  • Form delivery: Web3Forms, which forwards form submissions to our inbox. If it is unavailable, the website offers to open your own email app so you can send your message directly.
  • Email, documents and file storage: our business email and productivity providers.
  • AI tools: AI model providers used as described in section 4.

Some features link to services that we don’t control, and those services’ own privacy policies apply:

  • Language selector: choosing a language opens the page in Google Translate, which means Google receives the page address and content.
  • AI Visibility Check: the “Ask” buttons open your question in ChatGPT (OpenAI), Perplexity or Google. Anything sent there is shared with that provider, not with us.
  • Social links: LinkedIn, Instagram and X.

6. When we share information

We don’t sell personal data, and we don’t share it with others for their advertising. We share it only:

  • with the service providers listed above;
  • with a partner or specialist, such as an implementation or regulated-services partner, but only when you’ve asked us to make an introduction or agreed to bring them into your project;
  • when the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our users or the public; and
  • with a buyer or successor if our business is reorganised or transferred, in which case this policy continues to apply to your information.

7. International transfers

We are based in India, and our providers may process data in other countries, including the United States and countries in the European Union. When we transfer personal data, we rely on our providers’ contractual and security commitments and, where the EU or UK GDPR applies, on recognised transfer safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. We don’t transfer personal data to any country the Government of India has restricted.

8. How long we keep information

  • Inquiries and free report requests that don’t lead to an engagement: up to 24 months after our last contact, so that we have the context if you get in touch again. We delete them sooner if you ask.
  • Client records: for the length of the engagement and afterwards for as long as tax, accounting or other legal obligations require.
  • Website log data: kept by our hosting provider for its standard short retention period for security and operations.

When we no longer need information, we delete or anonymise it.

9. Security

We protect personal data with reasonable security measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls and reputable providers. No method of storing or sending data online is completely secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update it;
  • ask us to delete it, unless we have to keep it by law;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, which doesn’t affect anything we did before you withdrew it;
  • object to or ask us to restrict certain uses, and ask for a copy in a portable format (EU and UK residents);
  • nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated (India).

To make a request, email hello@marketcentaur.com. We may need to confirm your identity first. We aim to respond within 30 days.

If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live. In India, that is the Data Protection Board of India, which you can approach after first raising the matter with us. In the EU or UK, it is your local supervisory authority.

11. Cookies and similar technologies

We use Google Analytics, but only if you accept analytics cookies in the cookie banner. It helps us understand how visitors find and use our website, such as which pages are read and whether a contact form is sent. It collects information like the pages you visit, how you arrived, your approximate location, and your device and browser type. We use it only in aggregate, to improve the site, and never to identify you or for advertising.

If you accept, Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (whose names start with _ga) that last up to two years. If you reject or don’t choose, Google Analytics doesn’t load at all, so it sets no cookies and sends nothing to Google. Advertising and ad-personalisation features are switched off either way.

We remember your choice in your browser’s local storage for six months, then ask again. You can change it at any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer of every page, or here: change your cookie settings. If you withdraw consent, we delete the analytics cookies on our site.

We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and isn’t used for visitors in the EU. Our free tools don’t store your answers in your browser after you leave the page. Our hosting and security provider may set strictly necessary cookies to protect the site from bots and abuse.

We don’t use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If that changes, we’ll update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

12. Children

This website and our services are meant for businesses and adults. We don’t knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we’ll delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We’ll update this policy when our practices, providers or legal obligations change, and change the “Last updated” date above. If a change significantly affects how we use information you’ve already given us, we’ll tell you directly where we can.

14. Contact and grievances

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, email hello@marketcentaur.com with “Privacy” in the subject line. We handle every privacy grievance and aim to resolve it within 30 days.
