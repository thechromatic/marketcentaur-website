Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how MarketCentaur (“MarketCentaur”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you visit marketcentaur.com or contact us. Please read it alongside any notices shown when you use a specific service.

In brief: We use information you choose to submit to respond to inquiries and explore potential business engagements. Please do not submit sensitive personal information through this website.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, information may include your name, business email address, company name, company website, the service you are interested in, and the message you send. When you browse the site, the hosting provider or security infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, request logs and diagnostic data as part of operating and protecting the website.

2. How we use information

  • Respond to questions and business inquiries.
  • Understand your stated marketing needs and prepare relevant follow-up.
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the website.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and protect our legitimate business interests.

We do not claim that submitting an inquiry automatically subscribes you to marketing emails. If we introduce a newsletter or other marketing subscription, we will provide an appropriate notice and choice where required.

3. Contact form and service providers

The contact form uses a third-party form-processing service to relay submitted information to hello@marketcentaur.com. Your submission may be processed by that service and its infrastructure under its own terms and privacy practices. The website’s hosting and security providers may process technical request data to deliver and protect the site. Third-party providers operate under their own terms and privacy practices; please avoid submitting sensitive personal information through the website.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information as a business model. We may disclose information to service providers that help operate the site or handle inquiries, where legally required, to protect rights and security, or in connection with a business transfer. We aim to limit disclosures to what is reasonably necessary.

5. Retention and security

We retain inquiry information for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a prospective or existing business relationship, maintain appropriate records, and meet legal requirements. Retention periods depend on context. We use reasonable safeguards, but no website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. International processing

Because our website and service providers may operate internationally, information may be processed in countries other than where you live. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, those safeguards should be applied by the relevant parties.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, email hello@marketcentaur.com. We may need to verify your request and may retain certain information where lawfully required.

8. Cookies and analytics

This site is not described here as using advertising cookies or analytics tools. Hosting, security, or embedded third-party services may use necessary technical storage or logs. If we add analytics, advertising, or other non-essential tracking, we should update this policy and implement any consent mechanism required by applicable law.

9. Children

This website is intended for business audiences and is not directed to children. Please do not submit children's personal information through the site.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website, providers, or legal obligations change. The “Last updated” date indicates the latest revision published here.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@marketcentaur.com.

This page is general information, not legal advice. MarketCentaur should review and adapt it to its actual data practices and applicable jurisdictions before relying on it as a compliance document.
