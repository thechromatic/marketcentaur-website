What we measure

The five dimensions of AI readiness

AI projects rarely fail because of the model. They stall because one of these five foundations is missing.

1 · Strategy

Clear problems and owners

You know which business problems AI should solve, who owns them, and leaders have agreed the priorities.

2 · Data

Data you can trust

Key customer, sales and operations data lives in systems, with agreed definitions and reports that match.

3 · Processes

Work that is mapped

Main workflows are documented, and some repetitive tasks are already automated, so AI has something solid to plug into.

4 · People

Skills and habits

Most of the team uses AI weekly and knows what good, safe use looks like for their role.

5 · Governance

Rules and review

A written policy covers tools and data, someone reviews customer-facing AI output, and you know where AI is used.
