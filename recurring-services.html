How recurring work is scoped

1. Define the decision

Agree what the work should help you decide or improve, rather than monitoring activity for its own sake.

2. Set boundaries

Confirm sources, sectors, account volume, cadence, deliverables, access needs and responsibilities.

3. Review and adapt

Use regular reviews to assess usefulness, refine priorities and stop work that is not informing action.

Research updates include source context and suggested next steps. Privacy, outreach and platform requirements are considered as part of delivery, and scope is agreed before work begins.
