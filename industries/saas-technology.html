Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can you work with our CRM and sales tools?

Yes. We deliver account lists and research in formats ready for HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho and common sales engagement tools, and can help set up the workflows.

Do you write outreach sequences?

Yes. We provide segment-specific message angles and can draft sequences for your team to review and send. Nothing is sent on your behalf without approval.

How is this different from buying a contact database?

Databases give you contacts. We give you ranked accounts with a reason to talk to them now, the roles that matter and what to say, all with sources.

Have a sector-specific challenge?

Tell us what you're trying to improve. We reply within one business day with a suggested starting point.
