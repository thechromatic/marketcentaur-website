Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can you localize video courses?

Yes. We provide subtitles, translated on-screen text and AI voiceover, and keep terminology consistent with a course glossary.

Is student data safe with an AI assistant?

We configure assistants to collect only what's needed for follow-up, follow your data policies, and hand sensitive questions to staff.

Do you work with individual course creators?

Yes. Many of our content and localization services suit independent creators as well as institutions.
