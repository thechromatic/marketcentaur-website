Can AI provide legal advice?

No. AI can support internal workflows; qualified lawyers remain responsible for advice and review.

Can confidential documents be used?

We assess tools, permissions and data flows and recommend safeguards for your internal review first.

Can you draft an AI policy for a firm?

Yes. We prepare an AI policy and use register for review against your professional obligations.
