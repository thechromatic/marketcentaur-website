Can the AI front desk book site visits?

Yes. It can collect buyer requirements, offer available slots and add confirmed visits to your team's calendar, with reminders.

Does it work with property portals?

Inquiries from most portals arrive by email, phone or WhatsApp, and we route them into the same follow-up workflow so none are missed.

Can you create content for NRI or overseas buyers?

Yes. We can localize project content and run assistants in multiple languages and time zones.
