Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does the AI give medical advice?

No. Our assistants handle administrative questions such as bookings, timings, location and general service information. Clinical questions are always passed to qualified staff.

Is patient information protected?

We configure assistants to collect the minimum information needed, follow your data-protection requirements, and avoid storing clinical details in chat logs.

Can content be reviewed by our doctors?

Yes. All health-related content is reviewed and approved by your clinicians before publishing.
