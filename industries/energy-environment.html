Which regions can you monitor?

Do you provide ESG assurance?

No. We help structure data and narrative for reporting. Formal assurance and certification stay with qualified providers.

Can you help find EPC or channel partners?

Yes. Our partner discovery work identifies complementary firms by region, capability and project type.
