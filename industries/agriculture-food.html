Can AI process product and supplier documents?

It can extract and organise common information, with review steps for records that need confirmation.

Can dashboards connect operational information?

Where data is accessible and consistent, dashboards give one shared view of selected measures.

Will AI replace quality checks?

No. Human quality and food-safety processes stay essential; AI helps with the supporting information work.
