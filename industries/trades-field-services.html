Can urgent calls still reach me?

Yes. You decide the rules, for example forwarding emergencies immediately and taking messages for routine jobs.

Will it work with my job management software?

Many common tools can be connected directly or through automation. If not, bookings and messages can arrive by email or WhatsApp.

Is this worth it for a small team?

Often, yes, because small teams miss the most calls. Before you start, we'll help you estimate how many inquiries you currently miss and what recovering them would be worth.
