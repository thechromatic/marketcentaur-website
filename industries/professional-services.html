Frequently asked questions

Can you help a small firm compete with larger brands?

Yes. Smaller firms often win on specialization. We focus your positioning on the niches where you're strongest, then make sure your website, content and third-party profiles make that specialization obvious to buyers and AI assistants.

Do you handle confidential client information?

We work only with the information you choose to share, keep it confidential, and agree in writing how AI tools are used on your project. Client-identifying data can be excluded entirely.

Which starting point works best for professional services?

Most firms start with the B2B Account Intelligence Sprint to sharpen who they target, or an AI Search Visibility audit if inbound inquiries matter most.
